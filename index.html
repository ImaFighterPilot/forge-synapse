FAQ

Straight answers.

What's the difference between a chatbot and a multi-agent system?
+
A chatbot responds. A multi-agent system acts. A coordinator agent breaks down goals into tasks and delegates them to specialized agents — each with different tools, memory, and capabilities. The result is an autonomous workflow, not just a conversation.
How long does a full multi-agent system take to build?
+
Starter (1–2 agents): 5–7 days. Growth (3–6 agents + orchestration): 10–14 days. Enterprise: scoped individually. We give you a precise date after discovery — not a range.
Do I need technical knowledge?
+
No. You describe the business problem — we handle architecture, development, integration, and deployment. Your role is reviewing progress and approving milestones.
Which tools do you integrate with?
+
Any platform with an API: HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Slack, Notion, Airtable, Gmail, Shopify, Stripe, custom databases. If it exposes an API or webhook, we can connect it.
Is my business data secure?
+
Yes. Your data is never used to train AI models. We sign NDAs on all engagements. Enterprise clients can choose on-premise deployment — agents run entirely within your own infrastructure.
What happens after delivery?
+
All projects include post-launch support (30–90 days). We monitor agent performance and provide monthly reports. Retainer partnerships for ongoing optimization start at $1,500/month.
