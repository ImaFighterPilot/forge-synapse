FAQ

Straight answers.

What's the difference between a chatbot and a multi-agent system?
+
A chatbot responds to inputs. A multi-agent system executes goals. An orchestrator agent breaks a business objective into tasks and delegates them to specialized agents — each with distinct tools, memory, and decision logic. The result is an autonomous workflow that operates without human input.
How long does it take to build and what's a realistic payback period?
+
Starter systems (1–2 agents): 5–7 days to deploy. Growth systems (3–6 agents + full orchestration): 10–14 days. Most clients see positive ROI within 6–8 weeks of deployment. We calculate expected payback during the discovery call so you have a clear figure before committing.
Do I need technical knowledge to work with you?
+
No. You describe the business problem and the outcome you want. We handle architecture design, development, integration, testing, and deployment. Your role is reviewing progress at key milestones and approving before anything goes live.
Which tools and platforms do you integrate with?
+
Any platform that exposes an API or webhook: HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Slack, Notion, Airtable, Gmail, Outlook, Shopify, Stripe, and custom internal systems. We document every integration point in the architecture phase before building.
Is my business data secure?
+
Yes. Your data is never used to train AI models. We sign NDAs and data processing agreements on all engagements. Enterprise clients can choose on-premise deployment — agents run entirely within their own infrastructure with no external data transmission.
What does ongoing support and the retainer cover?
+
Post-launch support (30–90 days depending on plan) covers monitoring, bug fixes, and minor adjustments. The monthly retainer (from $1,200/mo) covers continuous optimization, new agent development, performance reporting, and priority response. Most clients move to a retainer once they see clear ROI.
